# Claude news runbook (scheduled task prompt)

Two scheduled runs a day post AI and cyber news to **@thecyberintellect** on Instagram and **@CISMNishant** on X. Each run publishes **5 stories: 3 reels and 2 carousels**. Nothing is scheduled in Metricool any more: its free plan publishes only 20 posts a month, kept for the CISSP Shorts. **Instagram** is published at each slot by the GitHub Actions workflow "Claude news Instagram" through Instagram's own API (`claude-news/ig_publish.py`, every 10 minutes); **X** is scheduled through Typefully by the "Claude news media" workflow when you push the run file (plus Threads/Bluesky if connected in Typefully). The account is new: reels are what reach non-followers, the first second decides whether anyone watches, and **shares (people sending the post to someone) are the strongest signal**, so every post is built to be sent. This is separate from the GPT pipeline in `the-cyber-intellect/` and from the CISSP reel pipeline in `render/`: never edit, move or delete their files.

Nishant is not present and wants no human step: never ask questions or wait for approval. Make reasonable choices and say what you chose in the final message.

## 0. Setup
- Attach the repo with push access (add_repo owner=`nishant1004sharma` repo=`cwn-reels` access=push), clone it (`git clone --depth 1`) and work inside it.
- `RUN` = `am` if Kuala Lumpur time (`TZ=Asia/Kuala_Lumpur date +%H`) is before 14:00, else `pm`. `DATE` = `TZ=Asia/Kuala_Lumpur date +%F`. Run file: `claude-news/content/$DATE-$RUN.json`. If it already exists and its items are recorded in `claude-news/state/posted.json`, stop and report "already done".
- Slots (Asia/Kuala_Lumpur): am = 08:15, 10:00, 12:00, 13:30, 14:30; pm = 16:00, 17:00, 20:00, 21:00, 22:30. Keep 12:00, 17:00 and 21:00 exactly (move a clashing neighbour instead): Nishant gets an engagement reminder about 17 minutes after each of those three, so put the strongest story of the run in one of them. (The am run fires at about 07:22, after GPT's pipeline has scheduled its day at about 07:04, so its picks are visible.)

## 1. Look at Metricool first (read only)
- `getBrandSettings`: blogId and connected networks in `networksData`: Instagram is `instagramData`, YouTube `youtubeData`, LinkedIn a `linkedin…Data` key. Post only to networks that are connected. X does not go through Metricool any more (see step 4).
- `getScheduledPosts` from 3 days ago to tomorrow (timezone Asia/Kuala_Lumpur). Use it to:
  - avoid clashes: other posts (GPT's run at about 09:00, 13:00, 19:00; the CISSP Short at 11:00 on YouTube and Instagram) must be at least 20 minutes from any of our slots. Move a clashing slot later in 15-minute steps.
  - avoid repeats: skip any story already covered by any post in that window, whoever made it.
- **Duplicate check against GPT (Nishant's rule: if GPT already has the story, change ours).** Also read, without editing, GPT's planned content: the files in `the-cyber-intellect/content/` for yesterday, today and tomorrow (e.g. `ls the-cyber-intellect/content/`, then the titles, captions and sources inside). A story counts as a duplicate if it is the same incident, vulnerability (same CVE) or announcement, even with a different angle. Replace every duplicate with a different verified story before writing the run file, and say in the final message which stories you swapped and why. This applies to X as much as Instagram.
- Any slot less than 25 minutes from now moves to now + 30 minutes (rounded up to 5), keeping the 20-minute gaps.

## 2. Pick 5 stories
- Window: news from the last 36 hours (am run: overnight US/EU news first). Use WebSearch (`extended` mode) with several queries, e.g. "zero-day exploited", "CISA KEV added", "data breach confirmed", "ransomware attack", "AI agent vulnerability", "prompt injection", "AI security", "agentic AI", "AI model release", "LLM jailbreak", "supply chain attack", "Patch Tuesday" (plus today's date).
- Mix in every run: at least 1 AI / agentic-AI story (security of AI, AI used in attacks, or a major AI release with a security angle), at least 1 exploited vulnerability or zero-day, at least 1 attack or breach. Fill the rest with the strongest stories.
- Skip anything in `claude-news/state/posted.json` (by `story_key`: the CVE id if there is one, otherwise a short slug) or already covered per step 1.
- **Verify every story with WebFetch** on a primary source (vendor advisory, CISA, government regulator, company notice, Microsoft/Google/Mandiant research) or established security press (BleepingComputer, The Hacker News, SecurityWeek, The Record, Dark Reading, CSO, Infosecurity, The Register, Reuters, AP). Every number, CVE id, CVSS, version, date and attribution must come from a page you fetched this run. Drop anything you can't confirm.
- Never name a victim from a ransomware leak-site claim alone. Attribution is "X says / attributes to", never stated as fact. No exploit steps, payloads or live targets: what happened, why it matters, what to do.

## 3. Write the run file
Follow the shape of the newest file in `claude-news/content/` (fields listed below win over older files). Items in this order: 1 = reel, 2 = carousel, 3 = reel, 4 = carousel (a diagram on Monday, Wednesday and Friday pm), 5 = reel. Put the strongest story first and the most visual / shocking stories in the reels. Ids `$DATE-$RUN-01` to `-05`. `slot` = `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:00` (KL time). `category` is one of: ZERO-DAY, PATCH NOW, VULNERABILITY, BREACH, RANSOMWARE, ATTACK, THREAT INTEL, SUPPLY CHAIN, AI SECURITY, AGENTIC AI, AI THREAT, AI NEWS, CLOUD, IDENTITY, POLICY, DEFENCE.

The voice that makes these engaging (this is the point of the whole pipeline):
- **Hook first**: lead with the most surprising concrete fact (a number, a time, an absurd detail), not the headline. "The alerts fired. A tool closed them as false positives." beats "BeyondTrust patches zero-day".
- **Practitioner take**: one line on why it matters to defenders, then concrete actions a security team can do this week.
- **End with one real question** that people can answer from their own experience.
- Short lines, plain words, no hype words ("game-changer", "chilling", "alarming"), no emoji walls (max 3 per caption).
- `x_text`: 270 characters max, no links, stands alone. Discovery on X runs on keywords: put the searchable names in the first line (product, vendor, CVE id, threat group, e.g. "VeloCloud", "CVE-2026-114625", "Storm-0940"), and end with 1 or 2 specific hashtags: one topic tag (#ZeroDay, #Ransomware, #AISecurity, #DataBreach, #PromptInjection…) plus #CyberSecurity or #InfoSec. Never @mention accounts (X restricts automated mentions).
- `ig_caption`: hook line, 3 to 5 "→" fact lines, the action(s), "Send this to <send_to> 👇", the question, a `Source:` line naming the outlet and date, then 4 to 7 hashtags (keywords people search, not generic tags).
- Carousel: `cover.hook` (max 90 chars) + `cover.sub`, 4 or 5 body slides mixing `point` (title + body max 260 chars), one `stat` ("BIG" + label) and one `actions` slide (3 or 4 items), plus `question`. Card: hook, sub, 3 bullets (max 95 chars each).
- Reel (13 to 20 seconds, built to loop): same schema as `bank/reels.json` segments (`say`, `show`, `card`, `card_style` default|stat|alert|fact|answer), 3 to 5 segments, **35 to 55 spoken words**. `reel.hook` max 45 chars. `reel.series` is exactly one of the named series people come back for: "Breach of the Day", "Patch Now in 15s", "AI Attack Explained", "Zero-Day Alert", "Myth vs Reality".
  - **Segment 1 is the hook, on screen and spoken from 0.0 s**: the most surprising number or fact in the first 3 to 5 words, with a `stat` or `alert` card. No warm-up ("Hey", "Today", "Breaking news", "In this video"). E.g. "Twenty-two hundred tenants. One stolen app." not "Microsoft has published research about…". Punchy never means inflated: "targeted" stays "targeted", never "hacked"; "attempted" stays "attempted".
  - Middle segments: one fact each, short sentences, ending on one action line (`answer` card).
  - Last segment: the sendable ending, spoken: "Send this to <send_to>." (e.g. "Send this to whoever approves your M F A resets."). Word it so it flows back into the hook when the reel loops.
  - `say` spells acronyms as spaced capitals (A I, M F A, I P) and never reads CVE ids or version numbers aloud (keep those in `show`/`card`).
- Every item has `send_to`: the specific person who needs to see this (max 40 chars), e.g. "your SOC lead", "whoever runs your help desk", "the person who patches your VPN". The carousel's last slide shows "↗ Send this to …"; the `ig_caption` has the line "Send this to <send_to> 👇" just before the question.
- (LinkedIn is not posted for now: skip `linkedin` and `li_text`.)
- **Sunday pm run only: weekly X thread.** On item 4 (the carousel), add `x_thread`: 5 to 8 posts, each max 270 characters. Post 1 is exactly `x_text` (the hook); then one post per key fact or lesson from the week's biggest story (it can draw on the week's other verified stories in `claude-news/content/`); then the actions; the last post asks the question and names the sources. The workflow posts it as a thread on X with the cover image on post 1.
- **Animated explainer (Monday, Wednesday and Friday pm runs only, Nishant's request).** Item 4 is `"kind": "diagram"` instead of a carousel: a silent, looping 4:5 video of a flow diagram whose dashed arrows move (like the "RAG vs Agentic RAG" style posts). See section 3b.
- Run `python3 claude-news/validate.py <run file>` and fix until it prints ok.

## 3b. Animated explainer (kind "diagram")
- Topic: one evergreen AI or cyber concept that explains *how something works*, ideally the mechanism behind one of this run's stories or a recent one (e.g. prompt injection vs a normal agent, RAG vs agentic RAG, MCP tool access, device-code phishing flow, ransomware double extortion, OAuth token theft, zero trust vs VPN, supply-chain attack via a package). Two panels compared ("X vs Y") or one flow with a good and a bad path work best.
- `story_key`: `explainer-<slug>`; never repeat one in `claude-news/state/posted.json`. GPT's pipeline (`the-cyber-intellect/content/`) also posts evergreen explainers: skip any topic it covered in the last 30 days.
- Facts in the diagram (how a protocol or attack works, recommended defences) come from a page you fetched this run (OWASP, MITRE ATT&CK, NIST, CISA, vendor docs, or the news source). Put it in `sources`. No exploit steps or payloads: show the flow at the level of boxes and arrows.
- Build it: copy `claude-news/diagrams/_template.html` to `claude-news/diagrams/<item id>.html` and edit the SVG. Keep the template's look: **clean white background, dark text, coloured outlines only, no gradients or photos**, so it reads on a phone. Title max 40 characters, text 20px or larger, max about 12 boxes, one idea per panel, a takeaway / defences box at the bottom, and the "↗ Send this to <send_to>" line at the foot. Animated arrows use `class="flow"` (blue), `flowG` (green, the safe path) or `flowR` (red, the attack path); `pulse` and `blink` highlight the key box. Don't add animations with other periods (the loop must stay seamless).
- Item fields: `diagram: {"title", "html": "claude-news/diagrams/<id>.html", "seconds": 8}`, plus `send_to`, `sources`, `x_text`, `ig_caption` (the caption explains the diagram step by step with "→" lines, ending with the send line, the question, `Source:` and hashtags).
- Preview before pushing: `python3 claude-news/diagram_render.py claude-news/diagrams/<id>.html /tmp/d.mp4` (needs Playwright), grab a frame with ffmpeg and look at it: nothing clipped, overlapping or too small.
- Commit the HTML file together with the run file. The workflow renders `diagram.mp4` (silent, 8 s loop) plus `diagram-cover.jpg`; Instagram gets it as a Reel with that cover.
- **X:** the Wednesday and Friday diagrams are the X posts for those days (`"x": true` on the diagram). The Monday diagram is Instagram only.

## 4. Render (GitHub Actions)
- Commit only the run file (`git add claude-news/content/$DATE-$RUN.json`, plus `claude-news/diagrams/<id>.html` on a diagram day), message "News run $DATE $RUN", `git pull --rebase origin main`, `git push`. The push triggers the workflow "Claude news media", which renders the cards and voiced reels (Nishant's ElevenLabs voice when the repo secrets exist, otherwise a neural voice) and publishes them to the `claude-news-media` branch.
- Wait for it: `gh run list -R nishant1004sharma/cwn-reels --workflow claude-news-media.yml -L 3` until the run for your commit completes (check every 30 s, up to 20 minutes). If it fails, read `gh run view <id> --log-failed`, fix the content if that's the cause, push again once, and wait again.
- Fetch the manifest through the API (the raw CDN can serve a stale copy after a re-render): `gh api "repos/nishant1004sharma/cwn-reels/contents/$DATE-$RUN/manifest.json?ref=claude-news-media" -H "Accept: application/vnd.github.raw" > /tmp/manifest.json`. Items listed under `errors` were not rendered: drop them. Check every media URL answers 200 (`curl -sIL`).
- **X budget (Typefully free plan, about 10 posts a month, resets on the 1st):** only items with `"x": true` go to X. Mark exactly ONE item `"x": true` on the Wednesday pm run (the diagram, item 4), the Friday pm run (the diagram, item 4) and the Sunday pm run (item 4, the weekly thread); every other item gets no `x` field. That is about 13 a month, a little over the free quota, so the last ones of a month may be skipped as "quota used up": that's expected. Still write `x_text` on every item.
- X: the same workflow schedules the marked story's `x_text` with its cover image or reel on X through Typefully (secret `TYPEFULLY_API_KEY`). Read the result: `gh api "repos/nishant1004sharma/cwn-reels/contents/$DATE-$RUN/typefully.json?ref=claude-news-media" -H "Accept: application/vnd.github.raw"`. Report what is under `scheduled`, `skipped` (e.g. quota used up) and `errors` in the final message; do not post to X any other way.
- Look at the media: download each item's first image (or a frame at 3 s of each reel with ffmpeg) and view it. Text must not be clipped or overlapping. If one item is broken, drop just that item.

## 5. Instagram goes out by itself
- Do not create any Metricool post. The "Claude news Instagram" workflow publishes each item at its slot through Instagram's API, using the media and `ig_caption`, and skips anything whose caption is already on the account.
- After the slot of item 1 has passed (if the run is still going), you may check it with `gh run list -R nishant1004sharma/cwn-reels --workflow claude-news-ig.yml -L 3`; otherwise just report the slots.

## 6. Record
- Append one entry per scheduled story to `claude-news/state/posted.json` `posted` list: `{"story_key", "id", "date", "slot", "kind", "networks": [...], "voice": manifest voice or null, "title": cover hook or reel hook, "typefully_draft_id": from typefully.json}` with networks `["instagram", "x"]`. Keep only the last 30 days of entries. Commit "Record news run $DATE $RUN", pull --rebase, push.

## Final message
Short bullets: the 5 stories (time, format, hook), networks posted per story, voice used for reels, anything skipped or failed in one line each.
